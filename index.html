About Vegas Fire Dancers

Where Fire Meets Community

Vegas Fire Dancers is Las Vegas, NV's premier weekly fire flow jam — a space where spinners, dancers, and performers of all experience levels gather to practice, learn, and create together.

Founded in 2026 with support from Fund the Flow Arts and sponsored by Pyrotechnique, we're building something special in Las Vegas, NV.

Community First

Inclusive, welcoming, and supportive of all skill levels, backgrounds, and prop types.

Safety Always

Trained safety personnel at every jam. Fire blankets, extinguishers, and first aid on-site.

Education & Growth

Workshops, mentorship pairings, and skill-building at every gathering.

Responsible Flow

Proper fuel handling, designated burn areas, and community guideline recommending no alcohol while spinning.

Safety First

Our Safety Commitment

Every jam in Las Vegas, NV is supervised by trained safety personnel. We follow industry best practices.

🔥

Fire Safety Crew

Dedicated spotters with fire blankets and extinguishers. Nobody spins without a spotter.

⛽️

Fuel Station Protocol

Designated fuel-up area away from the spin zone. Proper containers and spin-off towels provided.

🩹

First Aid Ready

First aid kit on-site with burn care supplies. Safety team trained in burn treatment.

📜

Community Guidelines

Shared guidelines for responsible spinning. Participants are encouraged to avoid alcohol while spinning, wear proper attire, and show respect for all.

🎓

New Spinner Orientation

First-timers get a safety walkthrough before touching fire.

📍

Designated Zones

Clearly marked spin zone, fuel station, spectator area, and cool-down zone.
